More than a badge.

What is JDM?

JDM stands for Japanese Domestic Market. Strictly speaking, it refers to vehicles and parts made for sale in Japan. A Japanese-brand car sold elsewhere is not automatically JDM.

Enthusiasts also use the term more broadly for a culture inspired by Japanese performance cars. Distinctive engineering, motorsport success and expressive styling helped these cars find fans around the world.

Why JDM Cars Are Popular

  • Tuning potential and iconic engines
  • A reputation for thoughtful, reliable engineering
  • Motorsport and rally heritage
  • Unique styling with a recognizable identity
  • A strong, worldwide enthusiast community
