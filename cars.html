01 / The garage

Built to be remembered.

Six icons. Six different approaches to performance. Get to know the machines at the heart of JDM culture.

01 / Nissan

Nissan Skyline GT-R R34

An all-wheel-drive performance coupe that brings together turbo power and sophisticated chassis technology. Its racing reputation and tuning following made it a JDM icon.

Notable engine: RB26DETT · twin-turbo inline-six

View specifications ↗

02 / Toyota

Toyota Supra MK4

The A80 Supra pairs a muscular coupe shape with a celebrated six-cylinder engine. Its tuning potential helped build a worldwide enthusiast following.

Notable engine: 2JZ-GTE · twin-turbo inline-six

View specifications ↗

03 / Mazda

Mazda RX-7 FD

A light, flowing sports-car design with an unusual rotary heart. Its compact engine and balanced handling give the RX-7 a character of its own.

Notable engine: 13B-REW · twin-turbo rotary

View specifications ↗

04 / Honda

Honda NSX

A mid-engine sports car designed to make high performance approachable. Its aluminum construction and high-revving V6 challenged expectations of a supercar.

Notable engine: C30A · naturally aspirated V6

View specifications ↗

05 / Mitsubishi

Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution

A practical sedan shaped by rally competition. The Evolution combines turbocharged performance with all-wheel-drive traction; the VIII represents its early-2000s era.

Notable engine: 4G63 · turbocharged inline-four

View specifications ↗

06 / Subaru

Subaru Impreza WRX STI

A rally-inspired sedan with a distinctive boxer engine layout. Its competition heritage and all-wheel-drive character made it a favorite among enthusiasts.

Notable engine: EJ20 · turbocharged flat-four

View specifications ↗
